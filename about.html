About

Retention is a craft. We treat it that way.

Kairos — from the Greek: the right moment. Every flow we build is about hitting exactly that.

Built by someone who reads the cohort tables for fun.

Kairos exists because of a pattern Mazz kept seeing inside DTC brands: beautiful stores, growing ad spend, and a customer list treated like a megaphone. Blast on Friday, discount on Monday, wonder why LTV is flat by Q3.

The fix was never more sends. It was better questions. When do customers actually go quiet? Which cohorts come back, and what did they experience that the others didn't? What is a missing win-back flow literally worth per month? Answer those with real cohort math — then let email and SMS do their job at the right moment — and retention stops being a cost center and starts compounding.

That's the studio in one sentence: AI-assisted analytics to find the moments, Klaviyo-native execution to meet them. No slideware, no vanity metrics, no interns learning on your account. Every engagement runs on the same playbook you see in the case studies — find the gap, price the gap, plug the gap.

— Mazz

How we operate

Four rules we don't break.

01

No blasts

If a message isn't triggered by behavior or aimed at a real segment, it doesn't get sent. Your sender reputation is an asset too.

02

Price every gap

Every recommendation comes with a monthly dollar value attached. If we can't price it, we don't pitch it.

03

Channel-native

Email persuades, SMS nudges, WhatsApp converses. Each channel does what it's best at — nobody gets double-pinged.

04

Prove it monthly

A report that ties every flow and campaign to revenue, every month. If the numbers stall, you'll hear it from us first.
