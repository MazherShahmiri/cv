The gap

Revenue looked healthy; the cohorts didn't. Subscribers churned hard at month 3, and the only "retention" running was a 20%-off blast every Friday — training customers to wait for discounts.

What we ran

  • AI cohort audit priced the month-3 churn cliff at $46k/mo
  • Churn-save flow: pause/swap offers before cancellation, not after
  • Post-purchase education series tied to the product's 90-day results window
  • Win-back at day 75, triggered by purchase-interval data, not a calendar
  • Retired the Friday blast; campaigns moved to a value-led calendar

The result

Email & SMS went from 18% to 43% of total revenue in 9 months — while total sends dropped 22%. Less noise, more money.

The gap

Subscribers cancelled the moment a bag piled up — and the brand only found out from the churn report. No skip option surfaced, no pause, no timing control: cancel was the only exit anyone could find.

What we ran

  • Skip-first save flow — skip, pause or swap roast offered before the cancel page, via SMS
  • Origin-story drops: monthly single-origin releases as a subscriber-only email series
  • Dormant win-back priced on purchase-interval data, not a blanket 90-day timer

The result

Churn down 31%, LTV up 44%, and the monthly drop emails hold a 71% open rate — subscribers now stay for the story, not just the beans.
