The problem

Every new subscriber got the exact same welcome email, no matter what kind of pet they had — dog owners were shown cat food, cat owners were shown dog food. The founder personally knew which product fit which pet, but that knowledge stayed in her head. It never made it into Klaviyo, and never made it into the one email every subscriber actually opens.

The gap

There was no segmentation inside Klaviyo at all — every signup looked identical to the system. The single highest-intent moment in the whole relationship, the welcome series, was being spent on generic content instead of the right product.

The fixes

  • "Which food fits your pet?" quiz as the primary signup unit — species, age, dietary needs captured as zero-party data
  • Welcome journey per segment: 6 branches, each with its own product logic
  • Replenishment flow timed to bag size and feeding rate
  • Bundle recommendations injected by segment into cart and post-purchase

The result

Flow revenue up 212% in four months. The quiz converts at 3.4× the old popup — and every completion makes the next email smarter.
