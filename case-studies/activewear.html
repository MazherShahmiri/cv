The gap

Years of buy-a-list-and-blast had put the domain one step from the spam folder: 71% inbox placement and falling. The best-designed email in the world earns nothing from a junk tab.

What we ran

  • Deliverability rescue: authentication cleanup, a warm-up schedule and a sunset flow that retired 40k dead addresses
  • Engagement-first sending: campaigns start with the 30-day engaged core and expand outward
  • Back-in-stock SMS for the chronically sold-out hero leggings — sized waitlists per SKU
  • Community drop calendar: launches as VIP moments instead of sitewide noise

The result

Inbox placement recovered to 98%, campaign revenue rose 89%, and every send now earns 3.2× what it did — same list, same products, working infrastructure.
